# Resolver-to-recipient provenance boundary

## Retained truths

The resolver must preserve stable unit and mine identity through movement, replacement, transformation, collision and death. Requested paid deployments need a disposition even when no unit materializes. The recipient producer must derive paid surviving/destroyed/wasted totals, replacement losses, visible observations, transient path reveals and memory from genuine causal events. It must not reconstruct witnessed facts from a later hidden simulator state.

The existing observation schemas validate recipient data relationships. They do not authenticate their producer or prove that a reported enemy loss was actually witnessed. The next resolver/producer bridge must establish that causal boundary before game/learning integration or training readiness.

## Canonical reference

`packages/starchess-core/src/resolve/provenance-types.ts` and `provenance-recorder.ts` at `b4b241481fb12de4a7344e6dba8eda775af57675` maintain engine entity IDs, action bindings, mutation effects, before/after frame images, visibility checkpoints, path-query traces and consumed-target proofs. The recorder binds mutable board piece objects to persistent identities and associates destruction with causal visibility. The proof/frame representation is distinct from canonical movement and collision rules.

The operator selected a new typed causal-event journal instead of preserving this companion representation. Use ordinary TypeScript types, Zod validation and standard JSON. Emit effects at actual resolver mutation sites with stable identities, action references, deployment dispositions and sufficient causal recipient-visibility evidence. Derive recipient lineage, receipts and replay without preserving old companion wire shapes or a bespoke authorization/ownership framework.

## Implementation boundary

The journal must preserve causal identity/visibility without copying the old frame/proof framework. Ordered mine selection is a game rule: first matching mine identity and removal of all mines on a spent tile must agree with canonical contact behavior. Preserve native mine order rather than sorting it away. Collision/path timing and actual operand identity determine who witnessed a loss. A final snapshot alone cannot establish those facts.

Deterministic contact/travel/ray prerequisites, the classical kernel and the complete B/C/F composer have canonical reference regressions. The new actual-mutation journal, bounded standard codec, receipt reduction and continuing recipient production now have persisted source tests. See [causal-journal.md](causal-journal.md) and [recipient-production.md](recipient-production.md). Runtime-owned isolation, independent-reference execution and native qualification remain pending. Source/test approval does not authorize game-data collection, training, further Docker execution, production host operations, publication or deployment.

## Retained causal privacy regressions

The reference recipient law in `packages/starchess-arena/src/challenge/r3Projection.ts` projects actual before/after mutation visibility and target-only successful native consumption facts. A speculative ray prefix is not an authorized view. Do not reveal incidental pieces/tiles encountered only in a hypothetical prefix, even when a later moved unit would have seen them there.

The replacement journal must preserve evaluation and actual operand identities separately. A successful native consumed target authorizes the actual applied target under the canonical guard; do not silently add an evaluation-ID-equals-applied-ID rule. Keep these IDs private. Geometric or consumed-target evidence must refer to the live actual victim in its causal pre-destruction state.

Order target assertions before the associated casualty and later visibility deltas. Clear consumed casualty memory before a later mine death, crush or hidden replacement; never restore a dead target from a delayed assertion. Record no consumed fact when the native application fails its live actor/victim guard or never fires. Retiring an own vision source must remove its light before later fire-and-forget losses are considered witnessed.

`packages/contracts/tests/recipient-privacy.test.ts` ports the behavioral fixtures from `packages/starchess-arena/src/__tests__/challengeProvenancePrivacy.test.ts`, not its old companion wire: hidden-prefix occupancy twins, retired-enemy-siege query twins, incidental moved ships, hidden paid replacement identities, casualty-memory clearing, nonacting-recipient isolation, failed applications and dead vision sources. Current-branch tests supply their own evidence. No historical companion-authority PASS transfers to this branch.
