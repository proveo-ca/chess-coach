### 1.2 Coefficient interpretation and authority

For every matrix row, a coefficient measures conditional score/logit sensitivity to that named supplied feature, holding other coordinates fixed.
Coefficients must be learned from the approved verified outcome/eligible-failure protocol, not assigned from prices, strategy scores, or historical winner labels.
With correlated features, its fitted value is not an isolated causal effect or an intrinsic value of a unit/mechanic.
No row assigns a coefficient value or guarantees its sign.
Native `UNIT_COST`, gas costs, prices, feature scales, Bayesian transition parameters, and geometric indicators are **not heuristic weights**.
Strategies are inspected objective inventories and possible frozen opponents, **not teachers**, labels, warm-start weights, rescue policies, or imitation targets.
`baselineScore` stays outside learned features and logits.

For a candidate-independent context term `a(h)`, score differences satisfy:

```text
[a(h) + w^T phi(h,c1)] - [a(h) + w^T phi(h,c2)]
    = w^T [phi(h,c1) - phi(h,c2)]
```

Shared context and bias cancel in both argmax and softmax.
Resource, material, uncertainty, and fog adaptation therefore need candidate × context interactions.
A linear ranker cannot recover omitted nonlinear unit×square×board relations merely by learning more carefully.
Equal vectors yield equal scores, with canonical key tie-breaking.

Native authority remains ultimate: shape/resource guards are hard rules; learned tactical risk is a different question.
Known-invalid intents must not become legal through a favorable coefficient.
An expressible fog intent may still fail native admission or settle differently from its nominal geometry.
The learned host selects once and stages once; it must not retry another candidate, salvage a prefix, or consult strategic rescue.

Model inputs exclude hidden world placement, actual enemy bank/resources, current enemy queue, private strategic KVS/runner/shooter state, enemy mode/mines, IDs, seeds, corpus/source metadata, future traces, and historical winner labels.
Own IDs may reconcile a future authorized ledger internally but never become numeric features.
Offline estimator truth labels stay in the separately approved fit/evaluation pipeline, never in policy histories.
