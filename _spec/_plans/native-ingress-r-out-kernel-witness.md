4. **Join preexec to POST-SUCCESS exec.** A separately trusted kernel-side witness, or an **independently qualified privileged** witness, must capture the selected opened object **before exec** and causally join its retained object lifetime/provenance to a kernel **POST-SUCCESS** exec of that **SAME opened object**. 5. **Prove coverage and delivery independently.** Arm and confirm coverage **before** the candidate event; cover all relevant CPUs and process transitions, and prove gap-aware completeness via independently qualified counters/sequence/loss detection, with no silent omissions.
