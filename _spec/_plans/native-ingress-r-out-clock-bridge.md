## Published semantics and checked local source

- Linux [`clock_gettime(2)`](https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man2/clock_gettime.2.html) documents nonsettable `CLOCK_MONOTONIC`, which excludes suspend, and nonsettable `CLOCK_BOOTTIME`, which includes suspend. [`time_namespaces(7)`](https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man7/time_namespaces.7.html) documents separate namespace offsets for both. Same host and same boot alone do **not** establish comparable clock values across namespaces. No suspend policy or clock ID is selected here.
- Rust [`Instant`](https://doc.rust-lang.org/std/time/struct.Instant.html) is opaque and specifies neither a portable serialized value/epoch nor suspend semantics. Its documented *current* Unix `now()` uses `clock_gettime(CLOCK_MONOTONIC)`, but that underlying call may change. Do not copy debug bits, reconstruct an `Instant` from a scalar, assume a common epoch, or silently equate it with a different clock domain. Checked subtraction/addition and explicit domain validation are necessary for any proposed bridge; saturating reversed-time subtraction cannot authenticate chronology.
- Checked local source: [`apps/arena/src/economic_client.rs:70-90`](../../apps/arena/src/economic_client.rs#L70-L90) creates each `Transaction` with a fresh `Instant::now() + 10s`; [`apps/arena/src/economic.rs:711-719`](../../apps/arena/src/economic.rs#L711-L719) replaces it at `next_transaction`. [`apps/arena/src/worker_transport.rs:104-135`](../../apps/arena/src/worker_transport.rs#L104-L135) records `started` at Node launch, then sets a 300s deadline and a work deadline 3s earlier. Neither is the original pre-launch F9 S0→Rust bridge.

## Two conditional proof shapes — neither approved

| Shape | Independent obligations; why the value alone fails |
|---|---|
| Original sampled S0 | A separately trusted source captures the **original authenticated S0 before every launch-causal action**; sole Rust owner receives it with proved same-boot, compatible time namespace/offset, explicit clock ID and suspend policy. Prove source, integrity, binding and bounded delivery, not just numeric equality or a boot ID. |
| Launch-bound origin receipt | An independently trusted issuer captures original S0 before all launch-causal work and authenticates a one-use receipt bound to issuer, session, role, sole owner, boot and clock domain, with freshness, bounded transport and anti-replay. A signed timestamp, signature or boot ID alone cannot prove **early capture**; the issuer/root/channel and its actual timing need separate human approval and causal proof. |

For either shape, owner entry `t_owner` is **not** S0. Delivering a fresh owner `now`, or computing a remaining duration *after delivery* without charging transit, renews the budget by transit time. Validate original domain, bounded transport and chronology; reject wrong clock ID/domain, ambiguous cross-boot or namespace/offset, forged, stale or replayed origin, reversed time, arithmetic overflow, expired cold gate or insufficient final cleanup time. Fail closed **before effects** at the deadline and before each owed operation. Synchronous F1 is not assumed preemptible: check the original S0+10s cold deadline immediately before **and immediately after** F1. If S0+10s has passed at the postcheck, revoke Native authority and prevent external publication; F1 may already have changed private pair state, and prior successful DIRECT charges persist without any rollback promise.
